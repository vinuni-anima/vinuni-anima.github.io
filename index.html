Our research group has adopted the name ANIMA, short for Adaptive Neuro-Inspired Models for Autonomy.

Project Overview

Advancing Humanoid Robot Learning through Perception Modeling, World Model, and Biomimetic Machine Intelligence

ANIMA is a research group at CAIR, VinUniversity. Our project explores how humanoid robots can learn internal representations of their surroundings and use them to anticipate what might happen next. We also consider applications to other autonomous systems, including advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS) and drones.

Research directions

Perception Modeling

Learn representations from sensory observations that help autonomous systems interpret their surroundings.

World Models

Build internal models that predict how an environment may change and what could happen if a robot takes an action.

Biomimetic Machine Intelligence

Draw on biological principles of perception, learning, and prediction to develop models for autonomous behavior.
